# Leadership

## Alexander Klyachko
### Managing Director

Alexander Klyachko co-founded Allied Testing in 2000. Alexander drives the strategic development and implementation of Allied’s business plans and long term future strategies and supports the day-to-day operations of the company. Alexander was one of the pioneers of the independent business in the former Soviet Union in late 1980’s — early 1990’s. Prior to founding Allied Testing, he set up Nidl, a group of companies providing retail financial services (1991). Since 2015, Alexander has been living and working in EU.

## Mikhail Teyf
### Director

Mikhail Teyf has been with Allied Testing from its inception in 2000. Mikhail plays a key role in the start of new projects and account teams, and oversees technical presales efforts. He has over 15 years of experience in the development, testing, and management of financial applications, and has previously served as Executive Director of Nidl (1996–2000) and as Head of the Automation Department at Northern Trade Bank (1992–1996). Mikhail received his MSc in Computer Science & Applied Mathematics from the Moscow University in 1987. Mikhail works at Cyprus office of Allied Testing and oversees its European accounts.
