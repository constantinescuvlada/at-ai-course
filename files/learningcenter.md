# Learning Center

Our people are Allied’s most valuable asset and their continuous professional development is one of our top priorities. Even though we strongly believe that there is no better way to learn than immersing into the real projects and getting hands-on experience, we do pay great attention to training. For this task we drew in company’s best experts with years of working experience who developed, designed and delivered an extensive program comprised of several dozen courses, varying in areas and levels. The wide range of topics covers every aspect of our work: software testing, software development, business analysis, project management, business communication etc.

As company is specializing in finance and trading, our training program is specifically tailored with focus on these disciplines and dedicated to cultivate deep domain knowledge. Over the last 15 years, hundreds of people have gone through Allied internal training. A few years ago we implemented certification system for employees who succeeded in training and passed end-of-course exams. The certificate confirms that the holder possesses proven knowledge and skills that meet industry standards.

When leaving Allied, certified individuals find themselves in high demand in the QA and testing career field. That is why we want to make it easy for certificate holders to provide verification of their accomplishments to current/prospective employers and clients.
