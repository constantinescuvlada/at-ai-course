Why it works

The docs in files/ — why Markdown?

Markdown (.md) is a plain-text format where a few symbols add structure: # starts a heading, - starts a list item, **bold** makes bold. The repo's files/ folder holds 25 such documents — one per topic of the company site. They are the bot's entire knowledge, stored as Markdown on purpose:

files/aboutus.mdFILE
# What We Do

**Allied Testing**, a company founded
in 2000, is a leading QA and testing
specialist firm with the main focus
on the **capital markets, trading
and finance industry**.

## Expertise

We have strong experience acting as
a **QA Center of Excellence** ...
  • Plain text — open and edit anywhere; no Word, no PDF software
  • Just enough structure — # headings and lists mark sections without layout noise
  • Easy for code — Python reads it as text and splits it into chunks for embedding; no parser needed
  • Clean embeddings — no page numbers or broken lines polluting the vectors (PDF extractions are full of them)
  • Git-friendly — the knowledge versions alongside the code, with readable diffs
